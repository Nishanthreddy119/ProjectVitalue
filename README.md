🚀 Presenting Vitalue 📱🎉 

Vitalue is an AI & ML project - a mobile app that estimates what you'll personally owe for medical procedure at a specific hospital, under your own insurance plan, before you get the care.

I spent 2 weeks on the data before writing a word of the proposal.  What I found, 32 Chicagoland hospitals publish usable price files. 888,146 price records across just four of them. Zero matching insurer names between health systems, until I wrote something to reconcile them. The data is public, It's just unusable. 

So I'm building Vitalue, a mobile app that tells you what an MRI or CT scan will actually cost YOU, at a specific hospital, under your own plan, before you get it. 

A few decisions I made deliberately: 
👉 📱 Mobile-first, because you need this answer standing in a clinic, not at a desk later.  👉 The market leader in this space has no mobile app at all. 
📊 A range, not a single number. Hospitals now publish 10th, 50th and 90th percentile prices.  
👉All arithmetic is deterministic code, and every figure the model writes gets validated against what the calculation actually produced.
